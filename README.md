# Minecarts Overhaul

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.11 that rethinks minecarts and rail transport.

Vanilla minecarts cap at 8 blocks/sec and furnace minecarts barely move. This mod reworks both through new rails with tiered speeds and a furnace minecart that actually self-propels.

- **Copper rails** — four oxidation tiers with maximum speeds of 40, 20, 10 and 5 blocks per second. Waxable with honeycomb, scrapable with an axe, oxidize naturally over time.
- **Furnace minecarts** — accept any fuel, face away from the placer, and propel themselves along their facing direction while lit.
- **Physics** — minecarts keep their horizontal velocity in the air and slide on ice.

The minecart experimental features are force-enabled so nothing else needs to be toggled.
